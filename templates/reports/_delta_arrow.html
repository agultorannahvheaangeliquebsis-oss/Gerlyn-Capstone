{% comment %} The direction mark inside a KPI delta pill, on the Dashboard rail and the Reports home rail. It used to be a ▲ / ▼ character baked into the pill's own text string back in reports/views.py::_delta_pill, which meant the view was rendering markup-free typography that only a template could size or align — the triangles sat on the text baseline and rode high against 13px/1.6 pill text. Splitting the direction into `delta.dir` leaves `delta.text` a plain sentence and lets .pill lay the mark out as the flex child it already has a `gap: 6px` for. Filled, not stroked, and deliberately unlike the line art elsewhere: this is not an object, it is the sign on a number, and at 10px a two-pixel stroke would close up into a blob.
