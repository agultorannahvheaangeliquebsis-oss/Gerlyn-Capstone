{% comment %} DESIGN.md §2c's panel row: what the sales are made of, and how they moved day to day. Plain .grid-2 — `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(320px, 1fr))` — so the panels lay out side by side and fold to one column on their own. That is also what absorbs a variable panel count: three or four children become that many columns on a wide screen and fold to two, then one, with no override, exactly as the Procurement tab's three-panel row does. The table follows at full width rather than sharing the row. It used to sit in the right-hand column beside a 340px donut; a second panel in that row would leave it in a column too narrow to read — the same trade the Procurement tab already resolved this way. Sales by Branch renders only when there is a split to show (see the view).
