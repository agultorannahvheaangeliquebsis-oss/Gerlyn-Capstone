M4 limitation, deliberately visible to the reader rather than papered over: there is no BillingStatusHistory table. A BillingRecord keeps only its CURRENT decision — confirmed_by / date_confirmed, and reject_reason while it is Rejected — and billing_confirm clears all three when a rejected payment is re-opened. So a payment that was rejected and then re-opened leaves nothing behind to list, and this block says so instead of inventing a timestamp, an actor or a transition for it. Every line comes from a persisted field (billing.views._billing_activity has the per-field reasoning) or from RefundEvent, which sales.refunds writes append-only and never edits. A real BillingStatusHistory model — every billing transition with its actor, time and reason — is a separate task, not something this template can fake. Refund decisions are Sales & Orders' to make: they are reported here, never offered here.
