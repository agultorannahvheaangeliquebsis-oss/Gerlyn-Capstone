{% t "How do I change my password?" %}

{% t "Go to Settings → My Profile → Password & Security → Change. If Two-Factor Authentication is on, we'll email you a secure link to confirm the change instead of changing it right away." %}

{% t "What is Two-Factor Authentication and how do I turn it on?" %}

{% t "It's an extra security step: once enabled, password changes must be confirmed via a link sent to your email, not just the current-password form. Toggle it from Settings → My Profile → Password & Security — you'll be asked to confirm your current password first." %}

{% t "How do I add or change my delivery address?" %}

{% t "Go to Settings → Addresses → Add Address. Pick your Region, Province, City/Municipality, and Barangay, then drag the pin on the map to your exact spot — the address text updates to match. Your default address is the one used for delivery; to change it, add a new address and set it as default." %}

{% t "What payment methods do you accept?" %}

{% t "Cash, GCash, Card, Bank Transfer, Cash on Delivery, and Pay at Store." %}

{% t "Do you deliver outside Baliuag?" %}

{% t "Delivery is free within Baliuag on orders over ₱500. For areas outside Baliuag, please reach out to your nearest branch (see the About page) to confirm coverage and delivery fees." %}

{% t "How do I control what notifications I get?" %}

{% t "Go to Settings → Notifications to turn order updates, promotions, delivery reminders, and the newsletter on or off individually." %}

{% t "Still need help?" %}
