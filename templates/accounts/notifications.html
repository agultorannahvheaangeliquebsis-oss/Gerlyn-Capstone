{% comment %} The preferences that govern the inbox, inside the card that shows it. This was a settings screen of its own — one rail row below "Notifications" and named a single word apart from it — so the answer to "why am I getting these?" was never on the page showing them. A drawer under the header, not a card of its own above it. As a separate card it opened ABOVE the button that opened it, so the whole Inbox card — header, pencil and all — dropped by the height of the panel on the click, and the control moved out from under the pointer that had just pressed it. Opening downward, nothing above it moves. Still not below the list: an inbox has no maximum length, so anything under it is buried the moment someone has a screenful of notifications. Folded away up here it costs the list nothing. Each switch is its own form posting one field, which is what lets a flip save on change without a Save button; the round trip is what confirms it, and the toast names the switch that moved. {% endcomment %}
