Help Center
How do I change my password?

Go to Settings → My Profile → Account Security → Manage. If Two-Factor Authentication is on, we'll email you a secure link to confirm the change instead of changing it right away.

What is Two-Factor Authentication and how do I turn it on?

It's an extra security step: once enabled, password changes must be confirmed via a link sent to your email, not just the current-password form. Toggle it from Settings → My Profile → Account Security — you'll be asked to confirm your current password first.

How do I update my contact number or business address?

Go to Settings → My Profile → Edit. Your contact person, mobile number, and business address are yours to update directly. Your business name, category, and assigned branch are managed by the store — contact your administrator to change those.

How do I respond to a purchase order or confirm a delivery date?

Open the order from your Purchase Orders list. Orders awaiting your response show a negotiation card where you can agree to or counter the proposed price and delivery date; once terms are agreed, you'll confirm the final delivery date there too.

Where do I see updates on my orders?

Go to Settings → Notifications, or the bell icon in the header — you'll be notified when GERLYN places a new order, approves terms, or records a delivery.

Still need help?

Contact your GERLYN store representative directly, or visit the branch you supply — listed on the About page.
