Delivery Areas

Where each branch delivers. An active province covers every municipality and barangay in it; an active municipality covers its barangays. The most specific entry for a place wins, so an inactive barangay or municipality is left out even inside an active province, and a specific entry’s fee and minimum order replace the broader one’s.
