Alerts Center

{% comment %} KPI cards, same as Reports and Stock per Branch — .label then .value, no inline tints. Colour is gated on a non-zero count, so an all-clear Alerts Center is a row of plain white cards rather than a red/amber wall that says nothing is wrong in the palette reserved for saying something is. Expired is .danger (already lost stock); the low-stock and expiring-soon counts are .warn (act soon). Approaching Expiry carries no tint: there is no info tint on a KPI card, and a 90-day horizon is an early-warning list rather than something to act on today. Floor Critical is deliberately not a card of its own. critical_count counts the low-floor-stock rows whose risk is Critical, and low_count counts every low-floor-stock row INCLUDING those, so the two nest rather than divide. The number is on the "Low Floor Stock" tab below, where it labels the table it opens instead of standing alone as a figure that double-counts its neighbour. {% endcomment %}
