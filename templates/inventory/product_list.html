{% comment %} Two groups, not five loose controls. The query is on the left — magnifier, field, and Scan as a trailing icon INSIDE the field's border, because scanning is another way of getting a code into that box rather than a button that competes with it. The magnifier is the submit — it already sat at the head of the field pointing at exactly that action, so a separate Search button beside it was a second control for one job. The filters are on the right, pushed there by .search-filters; they auto-submit, so they need no button of their own. .grow is gone deliberately: it stretched this field across the whole content column, which is what left every other control clumped against the far edge. The cap now lives in .toolbar-search .search-field. #product-search-input and #product-scan-btn are the hooks the scanner script at the bottom of this file binds to, and live-filter.js finds the field by input[type=search] within the form — all three survive the restructure. {% endcomment %}
