Released This Month is throughput, not a state anyone acts on, so it takes no tint. These three are not one series. Released This Month is a FLOW — throughput over a window, filtered on the release date. Pending Acknowledgment and Flagged Discrepancies are BACKLOGS — what is outstanding right now, filtered on status alone and deliberately unscoped by date.
