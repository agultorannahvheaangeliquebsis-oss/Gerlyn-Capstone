{% endfor %} {% comment %} The one KPI card (DESIGN.md §2), shared with the Dashboard rail and the Reports tabs. Reading order .label → .value names the figure before it is read. Tint is status, never category: .warn is "waiting on someone" and .danger is "act now", so both are gated on a non-zero count. Pending Acknowledgment is waiting on a branch to confirm receipt; a flagged discrepancy is someone's next job. Released This Month is throughput, not a state anyone acts on, so it takes no tint — it was the green card here, and green is reserved for the Dashboard's selected card. These three are not one series. Released This Month is a FLOW — throughput over a window, filtered on the release date. Pending Acknowledgment and Flagged Discrepancies are BACKLOGS — what is outstanding right now, filtered on status alone and deliberately unscoped by date. Subtracting the first from the second gives a number that means nothing. Do NOT "fix" that by scoping pending_acknowledgment to the month. A backlog has no natural window, and its oldest entries are its most important: a slip released in June and still unacknowledged is the one someone needs to chase. Month-scoping would drop those first, collapse the count on the 1st while nothing had actually been resolved, and — because the .warn tint is gated on a non-zero count — clear the amber overnight with the backlog untouched. The card would be announcing that nothing is waiting on anyone, and be wrong. The labels already carry the scope: "Released This Month" names its window, "Pending Acknowledgment" has no time word at all, which is how a present- tense backlog should read. The pending items are listed in the panel directly below, so the relationship is on screen either way. .compact because this row heads a table, not a graph panel, and three cards across the full width leave the default block padding as empty card. {% endcomment %}
