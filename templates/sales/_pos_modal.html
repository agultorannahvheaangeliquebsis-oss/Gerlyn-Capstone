{% comment %} Walk-in Sale, as a modal on the Order Console. It was its own page; the fields, their names and the JS below are the ones that page carried. This is the widest modal in the app (.modal-card--wide) because it holds a repeating item row rather than a column of fields, and it is the only one whose body is expected to grow while it is open — .modal-body already scrolls, and .modal-foot keeps Record Sale reachable no matter how many rows are added. Element ids are pos-prefixed. The console underneath has its own branch and channel selects in the filter toolbar, and the plain ids this form used to carry (branch, order_type) would have been ambiguous on a shared page. On a rejected submit the view sends back pos_restore_rows and the JS rebuilds those rows instead of the single blank one, so a cashier does not re-ring a basket because they forgot to pick a branch. {% endcomment %}
